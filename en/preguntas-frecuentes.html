FAQ

What we get asked most

Short answers about the firm and how we work. This is general information; for a specific matter, get in touch.

What is Culebro Abogados?
Culebro Abogados (legal name CG Legal & Real Estate Consulting, S.C.) is a corporate and real estate law firm with consulting, based in Los Cabos and Querétaro. We support foreign investment and Mexican companies with a business-minded legal strategy.
Where are your offices?
In Los Cabos, B.C.S. (Carretera Transpeninsular km 26, Palmilla, San José del Cabo) and in Santiago de Querétaro (Anillo Vial Fray Junípero Serra 4422, San Jerónimo).
Do you work in English?
Yes. We work in Spanish and English, with experience supporting foreign investors who buy, develop or operate in Mexico.
What practice areas do you handle?
Six: general corporate; real estate & hospitality; legal audit (due diligence); foreign trade; government & infrastructure; and philanthropic work. See Practice areas.
Can a foreigner buy property on the Mexican coast?
Yes. In the restricted zone (100 km along the border and 50 km along the coast), acquisition by foreigners is usually structured through a bank trust (fideicomiso) or a Mexican company. We review each transaction case by case.
Is Culebro Abogados a customs broker?
No. Customs clearance is handled by a licensed customs broker (agente aduanal). In foreign trade we advise, structure and ensure legal compliance, but we do not replace the customs broker.
How do you handle a matter?
With one person responsible per matter and close support in Los Cabos and Querétaro. We understand the business first and the legal problem second, so the legal strategy drives the operation.
How do I contact you?
Through the website form or at contacto@cglegal.com.mx. You can also message us on WhatsApp from the green button on the site.
