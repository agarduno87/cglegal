The firm

The firm, in its early days

Culebro Abogados —CG Legal & Real Estate Consulting, S.C.— is a corporate and real estate law firm with offices in Los Cabos and Querétaro. We support those who invest and operate in Mexico with a simple idea: understand the business first and the legal problem second.

How we work

  • One person responsible per matter, with close support.
  • Legal strategy at the service of the client's operation.
  • Bilingual service (Spanish and English) for foreign investment.
  • Presence in two key markets: Los Cabos and the Bajío.
